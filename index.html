Scavenger Hunt

Two hunters. Eight cute stops. One very adorable race.

How to play ♡

  • Each hunter opens this page on their own phone.
  • Use the same hunt code, and pick a different player.
  • Tap a card to flip it and read your directions and mission.
  • Write what you picked (at the photo booth, just take a picture).
  • Press Found it! to earn a stamp. Your friend sees it live!
  • Collect all eight stamps to unlock your haul card.

Both hunters type the same code. Use a new code for a brand new hunt.
